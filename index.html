How to Calculate Your Monthly Loan Payment

Your monthly loan payment depends on three main factors: the amount you borrow, the interest rate charged by the lender, and the length of time you have to repay the loan. A higher loan amount or interest rate will increase your monthly payment, while a longer loan term typically lowers it by spreading the cost over more months.

Understanding Your Results

Monthly Payment
The fixed amount you pay each month until the loan is fully repaid. This stays the same throughout the loan term.
Total Payment
The full amount you will pay over the entire life of the loan, including both the principal and all interest.
Total Interest
The extra cost of borrowing. This is the difference between your total payment and the original loan amount.

Frequently Asked Questions

What affects my monthly loan payment?
Three factors drive your monthly payment: the loan amount (how much you borrow), the annual interest rate (the cost of borrowing), and the loan term (how many months you have to repay). Changing any of these will change your monthly payment.
Does this calculator include extra bank fees?
No. This calculator estimates principal and interest only. Your actual payment may be higher if your lender charges origination fees, insurance, or other costs. Always check with your lender for the exact amount.
Can I use this calculator for a 0% interest loan?
Yes. Enter 0 for the interest rate to see your monthly payment when there is no interest. Your monthly payment will simply be the loan amount divided by the number of months.
