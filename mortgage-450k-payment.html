  4. Click Calculate — See monthly payment, total payment, and total interest.

What is the monthly payment on a $450,000 mortgage?

A $450,000 mortgage is a common size for home buyers. At 6.5% for 30 years (360 months), expect about $6.32 per $1,000 borrowed—so roughly 2,844 per month for $450,000. Use the calculator to compare 6%, 6.5%, and 7%.

This calculator shows principal and interest only. Your real monthly housing cost usually includes property taxes, insurance, and sometimes PMI. Budget for those separately. See our mortgage calculator for any amount.

Frequently asked questions

What is the monthly payment on a $450,000 mortgage at 7%?
Use the calculator with your quoted rate. At 7% for 30 years, expect roughly $6.65 per $1,000 borrowed per month in P&I.
How much do I pay in interest on a $450,000 mortgage?
At 6.5% over 30 years, you'll pay roughly 1.2× the principal in interest. A 15-year term cuts total interest significantly.
Does this include property tax and insurance?
No. This shows principal and interest only. Your lender will typically escrow taxes and insurance.
