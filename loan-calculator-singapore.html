Frequently asked questions

Does this work for Singapore loans?
Yes—the math is the same. Select the closest currency and enter your local amount and rate. The calculator models standard amortisation used by most banks.
Where can I get a loan in Singapore?
Banks, credit unions, and licensed lenders offer personal, auto, and mortgage products. Compare rates before you apply.
How long can I borrow for in Singapore?
Personal loans often run 1–5 years; car loans 3–7 years; mortgages typically 15–30 years. Check lender terms.
Will this match my bank's repayment schedule?
Close, but not always exact. Lenders may use daily rest, different rounding, or bundle insurance. Use this for planning; confirm with your bank.
Can I model interest-only or variable-rate loans?
This tool assumes fixed principal-and-interest over the whole term. Interest-only or variable products need separate calculations.
