What is the monthly payment on an $8,000 personal loan?

An $8,000 personal loan suits home repairs, medical expenses, or consolidating credit card debt. At 10% for 36 months, expect about $258 per month. Over three years you'll pay roughly $1,290 in interest. Shorten to 24 months and the payment rises to around $369—but total interest drops by about $450. Extend to 48 months and the payment falls to around $203, but total interest climbs by over $550.

Rates vary by credit and lender. Good credit might qualify for 8–10%; average credit often sees 11–15%. Online lenders fund quickly; banks and credit unions may offer lower rates to members. Compare APRs and origination fees. A 3% fee on $8,000 adds $240 to your effective cost—factor that into comparisons.

Frequently asked questions

How much is an $8,000 personal loan per month at 11%?
At 11% for 36 months, about $262/month. For 24 months, about $372/month. Enter your rate and term in the calculator.
Is 36 or 48 months better for $8,000?
36 months usually costs less total interest. 48 months lowers the payment—useful if cash flow is tight—but you pay more over time.
Does this work for an $8,000 debt consolidation loan?
Yes. Consolidation loans are typically unsecured personal loans. Use your quoted APR for an accurate payment.
