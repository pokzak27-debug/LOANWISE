What affects your $55,000 loan payment

  • Interest rate — Rates for personal loans often range from 6% to 36% depending on credit. A lower rate reduces both the payment and total interest.
  • Loan term — Shorter terms mean higher monthly payments but less total interest. Longer terms spread the cost but cost more overall.
  • Origination fees — If your lender charges fees and finances them, add the fee to the loan amount for a more accurate estimate.
