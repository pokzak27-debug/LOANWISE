What is the monthly payment on a $150,000 mortgage?

A $150,000 mortgage fits starter homes, condos, or smaller markets. At 6.5% for 30 years (360 months), expect about $948 per month in principal and interest. Over three decades you’ll pay roughly $191,000 in interest—more than the loan itself. Drop the rate to 6% and the payment falls to around $899, saving over $17,000 in total interest.

Rate changes matter at every loan size. A half-point cut on $150,000 saves roughly $50 per month and thousands over 30 years. Use the calculator to compare 6%, 6.5%, and 7%. Consider a 15-year term (180 months) if you can afford a higher payment: at 6.5%, about $1,306 per month, but total interest drops by over $120,000.

This estimate covers principal and interest only. Add property taxes, insurance, and PMI (if applicable) for your real monthly housing cost. See our mortgage calculator for any amount, or 30-year mortgage calculator to compare different balances at the same term.

Frequently asked questions

What is the monthly payment on a $150,000 mortgage at 7%?
At 7% for 30 years, about $998/month in principal and interest. At 6%, about $899/month. Enter your quoted rate in the calculator.
How much interest do I pay on a $150,000 30-year mortgage?
At 6.5%, roughly $191,000 in interest over 30 years. A 15-year term at the same rate drops total interest to about $82,000.
Does this include property tax and insurance?
No. This shows principal and interest only. Your lender will typically escrow taxes and insurance—add those to your budget.
