What is the monthly payment on a $20,000 loan?

A $20,000 loan suits major home improvement, wedding costs, medical bills, or consolidating higher-interest debt. At 10% for 48 months, expect about $507 per month. Over four years you'll pay roughly $4,340 in interest. Shorten to 36 months and the payment rises to around $645—but total interest drops by about $1,200. Extend to 60 months and the payment falls to around $425, but total interest climbs by over $1,400.

Rates vary by credit and lender. Good credit might qualify for 7–9%; average credit often sees 10–15%. Online lenders fund quickly; banks and credit unions may offer lower rates to members. Compare APRs and origination fees. A 5% fee on $20,000 adds $1,000 to your effective cost—factor that into comparisons.

Use the calculator to compare 36, 48, and 60 months at your quoted rate. Balance monthly cash flow with total cost. Frequently asked questions

How much is a $20,000 loan per month at 11%?
At 11% for 48 months, about $517/month. For 36 months, about $656/month. Enter your rate and term in the calculator.
Is 48 or 60 months better for $20,000?
48 months usually costs less total interest. 60 months lowers the payment—useful if cash flow is tight—but you pay more over time.
Does this work for a $20,000 personal or business loan?
Yes. The math is identical for personal, business, or general-purpose term loans with standard amortization. Use your quoted APR.
