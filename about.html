About LoanWise

Last updated: April 2026

Advertisement

LoanWise is a free online calculator hub focused on borrowing, repayment, and interest—built so you can plan with confidence before you talk to a lender or change your budget.

Our mission

We believe everyday financial decisions deserve clear numbers, not hidden surprises. LoanWise exists to put professional-grade payment math in your hands: you enter a few inputs, and you see how principal, rate, and time shape what you will owe. Our mission is to reduce confusion around loans and interest so shoppers, homeowners, small-business owners, and students of personal finance can compare scenarios honestly and move forward with a calmer, better-informed plan.

We also see calculators as part of a wider toolkit for financial and personal well-being. Sound borrowing fits alongside understanding your cash flow, your take-home pay, and the habits that support long-term stability—including health and fitness choices that affect your energy and focus. LoanWise today delivers deep coverage in loan and interest math; over time we aim to expand into adjacent areas such as salary and wellness-oriented planning tools, always with the same standards of transparency and respect for your time.

Why we built this

Too many people sign financing agreements after seeing only a monthly payment line on a screen, without grasping total interest, the trade-offs of a longer term, or how a slightly higher APR compounds over years. We built LoanWise to close that gap with fast, no-friction tools you can use privately on any device—no account wall, no email gate—so you can rehearse questions and compare offers on your own terms before you commit real money.

Our values

  • Accuracy. We use standard amortization and interest conventions and explain what the numbers mean. We encourage you to treat results as strong estimates for planning, then confirm details with your lender, tax advisor, or other professionals when decisions matter.
  • Simplicity. Busy people should not need a spreadsheet course to model a car loan or mortgage payment. Labels stay plain, flows stay short, and supporting articles—like our guide to how monthly payments are calculated—are here when you want to go deeper.
  • Free access. Core calculators stay free to use. We may show ads to help cover hosting and development; you can read how that works in our privacy policy. We do not charge you to click “calculate.”

Our tools

LoanWise’s live catalog centers on loans and interest—the math most people encounter when they finance a vehicle, consolidate debt, buy a home, or run a small business. Tools are organized so you can start broad and drill into specialized pages when you need regional context or extra scenarios.

  • General loan payment: Home loan calculator — monthly payment, total interest, and total paid with multiple currency formats.
  • Auto & car: Car loan, Auto loan, Used car, Trade-in.
  • Housing & large loans: Mortgage, Personal loan, Business loan.
  • Interest & learning: Interest calculator, Simple interest, Payment example, How payments work.
  • Regional calculators: USA, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE, and country comparison.
  • Advanced loan scenarios: Down payment, Extra payments, Interest breakdown.

We are actively growing the site toward a fuller suite of personal-finance and fitness-adjacent calculators—for example, take-home pay estimators and simple wellness metrics that pair with budgeting—so visitors can rely on one consistent, trustworthy experience across more of life’s “what if” questions.
