What is the monthly payment on a $400,000 mortgage?

A $400,000 mortgage fits higher-priced homes in many markets. At 6.5% for 30 years (360 months), expect about $2,528 per month in principal and interest. Over three decades you'll pay roughly $510,000 in interest—more than the loan itself. Drop the rate to 6% and the payment falls to around $2,398, saving over $47,000 in total interest.

Rate differences add up quickly on larger balances. A half-point cut on $400,000 saves roughly $130 per month and tens of thousands over 30 years. Use the calculator to compare 6%, 6.5%, and 7%. A 15-year term at 6.5% yields about $3,484 per month—higher payment, but total interest drops by over $320,000.

This estimate covers principal and interest only. Your real monthly housing cost includes property taxes, insurance, and sometimes PMI if your down payment is under 20%. Budget for those separately. Frequently asked questions

What is the monthly payment on a $400,000 mortgage at 7%?
At 7% for 30 years, about $2,661/month in principal and interest. At 6%, about $2,398/month. Enter your quoted rate in the calculator.
How much interest do I pay on a $400,000 30-year mortgage?
At 6.5%, roughly $510,000 in interest over 30 years. A 15-year term at the same rate drops total interest to about $219,000.
Does this include PMI?
No. This shows principal and interest only. If your down payment is under 20%, you may pay PMI—add it to your budget.
