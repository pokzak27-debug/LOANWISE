What is the monthly payment on a $40,000 car loan?

A $40,000 car loan fits SUVs, trucks, or well-equipped sedans. At 6.5% for 72 months, expect about $678 per month. Over six years you’ll pay roughly $8,800 in interest. Shorten to 60 months and the payment rises to around $783—but total interest drops by over $2,000. Rate differences matter more at this size: a drop from 8% to 6% saves about $2,800 in interest over 72 months.

Larger loans often come with 72- or 84-month terms to keep payments manageable. Longer terms mean more total interest and more time underwater. Use the calculator to compare 60 vs 72 months at your quoted rate. Manufacturer incentives—including 0% or low APRs—can significantly cut cost; enter the promo rate to see the impact.

Budget for insurance and maintenance on a $40,000 vehicle. Full coverage can add $150–300 per month. Run the numbers before you commit. Our car loan calculator works for any amount; this page is pre-filled for $40,000. See how much car loan can I afford for budget guidelines.

Frequently asked questions

What is the monthly payment on a $40,000 car loan at 7%?
At 7% for 72 months, about $691/month. At 6% for 60 months, about $773/month. Enter your rate and term in the calculator.
Is 72 months too long for a $40,000 car loan?
Longer terms lower the payment but increase total interest and time underwater. Compare 60 vs 72 months in the calculator—the interest difference can exceed $2,000.
Does this work for a $40,000 truck or SUV loan?
Yes. The math is identical for any vehicle. Enter your quoted rate and term for an accurate payment.
