What is the monthly payment on a $3,000 personal loan?

A $3,000 personal loan is an unsecured amount often used for repairs, medical bills, or small emergencies. At 12% for 24 months, expect about $141 per month. Over two years you'll pay roughly $395 in interest. Shorten to 12 months and the payment rises to around $267—but total interest drops by about $150. Personal loans in this range typically run 12–36 months.

Rates vary by credit. Good credit might qualify for 9–11%; average or fair credit often sees 12–18%. Online lenders and credit unions offer small personal loans with quick funding. Compare APRs and check for origination fees. Use the calculator with your quoted rate to see the real payment and total cost before you apply.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a $3,000 personal loan per month at 15%?
At 15% for 24 months, about $145/month. For 12 months, about $270/month. Enter your quoted rate and term in the calculator.
Can I get a $3,000 personal loan with bad credit?
Some lenders offer small personal loans to lower-credit borrowers, but rates are usually higher. Use your prequalified rate in the calculator.
Is 12 or 24 months better for $3,000?
12 months usually saves total interest. 24 months lowers the payment—better if cash flow is tight—but you pay more over time.
