Why use a Morocco loan calculator

Use this free loan calculator to model MAD payments for crédit à la consommation, vehicle finance, or crédit immobilier. See your monthly mensualité and total interest in seconds—ideal before you visit your bank.

Plan your dirham budget

A MAD 200,000 car loan at 6.5% over 4 years ≈ MAD 4,755/month. A MAD 1.5M mortgage at 5% over 20 years ≈ MAD 9,899/month. Use our car loan or mortgage calculator for product-specific views.

Compare conventional vs participative

Conventional banks use classic interest; participative (Islamic) windows use distinct contracts. If your bank still quotes equal monthly payments, you can model cash flow here. See our personal loan calculator and interest calculator for more.

Consumer and housing credit in Morocco

Moroccan banks offer crédit à la consommation, vehicle finance, and crédit immobilier mainly in dirhams (MAD), under supervision from Bank Al-Maghrib. Many conventional loans amortise like elsewhere: fixed mensualités that blend interest and principal on the remaining balance.

Participative (Islamic) windows use distinct contracts; if your institution still quotes equal monthly payments over a set horizon, you can map that here for planning, then validate every line with your adviser.

What influences your offer

Banks look at salary, employer stability, existing debt, and collateral. Real-estate loans may hinge on appraisal, down payment, and insurance. Published rate tables move with regulation and competition, so treat any online range as background noise until you have a personalised simulation from the branch.

Small changes in rate or term shift your mensualité noticeably on long mortgages—experiment with this tool before you sign.

Using MAD inputs correctly

Enter the net amount financed after any apport on a car or home. Bundle administrative fees into principal only if the bank capitalises them. Choose MAD in the currency selector for familiar formatting.

Frequently asked questions

Are loans always denominated in MAD?
Retail borrowers almost always deal in dirhams. Specialty professional products may differ—read your offer.
How long can terms run?
Consumer and auto credit often spans one to six years; mortgages may reach roughly 20–25 years depending on age ceilings and debt ratios.
Does this replace my bank simulation?
No. Insurance, file fees, and rounding can change the official schedule. Use LoanWise for orientation only.
How do participative products compare?
They follow Sharia-compliant economics. If you receive a fixed instalment amount, you can still preview cash flow here, but the legal meaning of “rate” differs.
Can I include optional insurance?
If insurance is financed, add it to the principal field; if paid upfront, keep it out and remember the extra cash outlay.
