What is the monthly payment on a $12,000 loan?

A $12,000 loan fits home improvements, wedding costs, or consolidating credit card debt. At 9% for 48 months, expect about $299 per month. Over four years you’ll pay roughly $2,350 in interest. Shorten to 36 months and the payment rises to around $382—but total interest drops by about $550. Extend to 60 months and the payment falls to around $249, but total interest climbs by over $700.

Personal loan rates typically run 7–18% depending on credit. Online lenders often approve quickly; banks and credit unions may offer lower rates to members. Compare APRs and watch for origination fees. A 4% fee on $12,000 adds $480—effectively raising your cost. If fees are financed, add them to the principal in the calculator.

Use the calculator to compare 36, 48, and 60 months at your quoted rate. Balance monthly cash flow with total cost. Our loan calculator works for any amount; this page is pre-filled for $12,000. For unsecured borrowing, see the personal loan calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a $12,000 loan per month at 10%?
At 10% for 48 months, about $304/month. For 36 months, about $387/month. Enter your rate and term in the calculator.
Is 48 or 60 months better for $12,000?
48 months usually costs less total interest. 60 months lowers the payment—helpful if budget is tight—but you pay more over time.
Does this work for a $12,000 debt consolidation loan?
Yes. Consolidation loans are typically personal loans. The math is the same—use your quoted APR and term.
