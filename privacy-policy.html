Frequently asked questions

Does LoanWise collect my loan amounts or personal data?
No. Calculators run in your browser. We don't store amounts, rates, or results. We may collect anonymized usage data (pages viewed, device type) to improve the site.
Why might I see ads or cookies?
We may use ad partners to keep the site free. They can set cookies and follow their own policies. You can adjust cookie settings in your browser.
How do I opt out or delete my data?
Since we don't require accounts or store personal loan data, there's nothing to delete. To stop tracking, use browser privacy settings or stop visiting the site.
