What is the monthly payment on a $15,000 loan?

A $15,000 loan suits home improvements, wedding costs, or consolidating higher-interest debt. At 8.5% for 48 months, your payment is about $369 per month. Over four years you pay roughly $2,700 in interest. Shorten to 36 months and the payment rises to around $474—but total interest drops by about $600.

Personal loan terms for $15,000 often run 36–60 months. Longer terms ease the monthly burden but increase total cost. A 60-month loan at 9% adds another $800+ in interest compared to 48 months. Use the calculator to see the exact difference at your rate. Compare offers from multiple lenders—a point or two in rate can save hundreds.

Secured options like a home equity loan may offer lower rates if you own property. Unsecured personal loans don’t require collateral but typically have higher rates. Our loan calculator works for any amount; personal loan calculator is tailored to unsecured borrowing. This page is pre-filled for $15,000 so you can quickly model different scenarios.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a $15,000 loan per month at 8%?
At 8% for 48 months, about $366/month. For 36 months, about $470/month. Use the calculator with your quoted rate and term.
Is 48 or 60 months better for a $15,000 loan?
48 months usually saves total interest. 60 months lowers the payment—useful if cash flow is tight—but you pay more over time.
Can I use this for a $15,000 car down payment loan?
