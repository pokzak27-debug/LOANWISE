What is the monthly payment on a $20,000 business loan?

A $20,000 business loan fits equipment purchases, working capital, or small expansion projects. Term loans in this range often run 24–60 months. At 9.5% for 48 months, expect about $503 per month. Over four years you’ll pay roughly $4,140 in interest. Extend to 60 months and the payment drops to around $420—but total interest rises to over $5,200.

Business loan rates vary by lender, credit, and business age. Banks and credit unions may offer 7–10% for established businesses; online lenders often range 9–18%. SBA loans can have lower rates but longer approval times. Use the calculator with your quoted rate to model different terms and see how they affect cash flow.

Plan for the payment in your cash flow. A $500 monthly obligation needs to fit comfortably alongside rent, payroll, and other expenses. Frequently asked questions

How much is a $20,000 business loan per month at 10%?
At 10% for 48 months, about $507/month. For 36 months, about $645/month. Enter your quoted rate and term in the calculator.
Is 48 or 60 months better for a $20,000 business loan?
48 months usually saves total interest. 60 months lowers the payment and frees cash flow—useful if the business needs breathing room—but costs more over time.
Does this work for equipment financing?
Yes, if the equipment loan uses standard amortization (fixed monthly payments). Some equipment leases use different structures—confirm with your lender.
