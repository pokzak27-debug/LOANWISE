What is the monthly payment on a $7,000 loan?

A $7,000 loan suits home repairs, medical expenses, or consolidating smaller debts. At 10% for 36 months, expect about $226 per month. Over three years you’ll pay roughly $1,130 in interest. Extend to 48 months and the payment drops to around $178—but total interest rises by about $400. Shorter terms save interest but require a higher monthly payment.

Personal loan rates typically range from 7% to 24% depending on credit. Online lenders often fund within a day or two; banks and credit unions may offer lower rates for existing customers. Compare APRs and check for origination fees. A 5% fee on $7,000 adds $350 to your effective cost.

Use the calculator to compare 24, 36, and 48 months at your likely rate. Balance monthly affordability with total interest. Our loan calculator works for any amount; this page is pre-filled for $7,000. For unsecured borrowing context, see the personal loan calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a $7,000 loan per month at 12%?
At 12% for 36 months, about $233/month. For 24 months, about $329/month. Enter your rate and term in the calculator.
Is 36 or 48 months better for $7,000?
36 months usually costs less total interest. 48 months lowers the payment—useful if budget is tight—but you pay more over the life of the loan.
